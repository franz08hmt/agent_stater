# Sunrise Family Clinic — Patient Guide (2026 Edition)

## 1. About Sunrise

Sunrise Family Clinic has two branches: the District 1 branch (25 Le Loi Street) and the Thu Duc branch (180 Vo Van Ngan Street). Information in this guide applies to both branches unless a section says otherwise. Section 10 (Updates) overrides earlier sections where they conflict.

## 2. Opening Hours

| Day              | District 1 branch | Thu Duc branch |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | -------------- |
| Monday to Friday | 7:30 – 19:00      | 8:00 – 17:00   |
| Saturday         | 8:00 – 12:00      | 8:00 – 16:00   |
| Sunday           | Closed            | 8:00 – 12:00   |

Sunrise is not an emergency center. In an emergency, call 115 or go to the nearest hospital.

## 3. Booking and Cancellation

Book through the SunriseCare app or the hotline 1900 6868. Appointments must be cancelled at least 4 hours in advance. A late cancellation or a missed appointment is charged a no-show fee of 100,000 VND.

## 4. Consultation Fees

| Service                                          | Fee         |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| General consultation                             | 250,000 VND |
| Pediatrics consultation                          | 400,000 VND |
| Dermatology consultation                         | 450,000 VND |
| Cardiology consultation (District 1 branch only) | 600,000 VND |
| Telehealth video consultation                    | 180,000 VND |

A follow-up visit for the same issue within 7 days of the first visit is free. This applies to in-person visits only. Telehealth follow-ups are charged the full telehealth fee.

Children under 6 years old get 20% off general consultations.

## 5. Insurance

Sunrise offers direct billing for Bao Viet and PVI insurance. For other insurers, the patient pays first and receives an invoice to claim from their insurer. Telehealth consultations are never covered by direct billing.

## 6. Test Results

Blood test results are available in the SunriseCare app within 24 hours. Imaging results (X-ray, ultrasound) are available within 48 hours.

## 7. Vaccination

Vaccinations for children under 6 years old are given on Saturday mornings at the District 1 branch. The Thu Duc branch also gives children's vaccinations on Sunday mornings. Parents must bring the child's vaccination booklet.

## 8. Sunrise Plus Membership

Sunrise Plus membership costs 600,000 VND per year. Members get:

- 5% off at the on-site pharmacy,
- 10% off all in-person consultations (telehealth is not included), and
- the first no-show fee of each calendar year waived.

## 9. Pharmacy

Each branch has an on-site pharmacy that is open during that branch's opening hours.

## 10. Updates (effective 1 October 2026)

The following changes override earlier sections of this guide:

1. The dermatology consultation fee is increased to 500,000 VND. Other consultation fees do not change.
2. Bao Viet direct billing is suspended at the Thu Duc branch until further notice. Bao Viet patients at the Thu Duc branch pay first and receive an invoice. Direct billing for Bao Viet continues at the District 1 branch.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I choose my doctor?**
Yes. When booking in the SunriseCare app, you can choose any available doctor at your branch.

**Is there parking?**
The District 1 branch has motorbike parking only. The Thu Duc branch has motorbike and car parking.

**Can I get my results printed?**
Yes. Ask at the reception desk of the branch where you had the test.
